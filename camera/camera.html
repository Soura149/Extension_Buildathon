How to use air-writing

  1. Show one complete, open hand inside the dashed box and hold it still until the camera is armed.
  2. Keep your index finger extended. The yellow cursor follows the fingertip.
  3. Bring your thumb and index fingertip together and hold the pinch briefly to start drawing.
  4. Release the pinch to stop. If your hand leaves the frame, show the open hand again to re-arm.

Use steady lighting, keep your whole hand visible, and avoid starting with a pinch. The first pinch after opening the camera is intentionally ignored until the open-hand pose is detected.

How to use air-writing

  1. Point with only your index finger ☝️ to write continuously at the fingertip.
  2. Pinch your thumb and index finger, or raise two fingers ✌️, to hover and reposition without drawing.
  3. Show an open palm ✋ to erase annotations around the palm and wrist center.

Use steady lighting and keep your hand inside the dashed calibration box. The cursor follows the active gesture smoothly; hovering never commits ink.
